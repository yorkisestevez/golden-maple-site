The Builder's Manifesto

We Don't Accept Standard.

The landscaping industry in Ontario is broken. It is defined by low bids, thin foundations, and disappearing contractors. We built Golden Maple to be the antidote.

A Message from the Founder.

"A patio shouldn't heave after one winter. A retaining wall shouldn't lean in five years."

When I started Golden Maple Landscaping, I noticed a disturbing trend in Simcoe County. Homeowners were spending tens of thousands of dollars on outdoor living spaces that looked beautiful on day one, only to watch them sink and shift just a few seasons later.

The reason was always the same: contractors cutting corners beneath the surface where the client couldn't see. Using 6-inch bases when 12 inches were required. Using cheap gravel instead of open-graded aggregate.

I decided we would build differently. We engineered a standard that exceeds municipal codes. Every project we undertake sits on a 12 to 16-inch open-graded base. This is the only way to guarantee the structural permanence your investment deserves.

— Yorkis
