Why We Include Permits & Engineering
We include permits, engineering, and coordination because they’re not optional add-ons — they’re what make a deck safe, insurable, and built to last.
In Ontario, deck height, structure, and load paths often require proper footing design, engineered structural sizing, and municipal review.
Rather than presenting these as surprise costs later, we account for them up front so your planning range reflects the real investment required to build your project correctly.
Our goal isn’t to be the cheapest option — it’s to be the most complete and dependable one.
