Interlock Patio Installation in Barrie, Ontario

Professional interlock patio installation built for Ontario weather and designed for everyday enjoyment.

A professionally installed interlock patio enhances outdoor living while increasing property value and long-term durability.

In Barrie's freeze-thaw climate, proper base preparation and drainage planning are essential for preventing shifting and settling.

Golden Maple Landscaping builds patios engineered for Ontario weather and designed for everyday enjoyment.

Built for Ontario Weather

Ontario's seasonal temperature swings place significant stress on outdoor surfaces.

Our patios are constructed using:

  • deep compacted aggregate bases
  • proper grading for water management
  • precision installation techniques
  • durable materials suited for freeze-thaw cycles

This ensures lasting performance through harsh winters and seasonal changes.

Common Problems We Solve

Homeowners often contact us due to:

  • sinking or shifting patios
  • pooling water near foundations
  • uneven surfaces creating safety concerns
  • outdated or unusable outdoor spaces

We address the underlying issues to create safe, functional, and attractive outdoor environments.
