Outdoor Lighting in Midhurst, thoughtfully designed and built

We create lighting plans that extend outdoor living with calm, reliable illumination and architectural clarity.

Lighting is planned to complement the hardscape and planting layout. We focus on subtle layers that guide movement and highlight key elements without creating harsh hotspots.

Our installs prioritize tidy wiring routes, durable connections, and fixtures placed with long-term maintenance in mind.

What we build

  • Path and step lighting for safe circulation.
  • Feature lighting for trees and architectural elements.
  • Ambient patio lighting for evening use.
  • Edge lighting for grade changes and transitions.
  • Discreet transformer and wiring placement.
  • Fixture planning aligned with the landscape design.

Our process

  1. Structured intake to confirm fit and project goals.
  2. Lighting plan and fixture strategy built into the layout.
  3. Installation with clean routing and final nighttime calibration.
