Thoughtfully Built Outdoor Spaces

We plan and build outdoor spaces with care, attention to detail, and long-term durability in mind. Our focus is simple — do the work properly and build things that last.

Built on Craftsmanship, Integrity, and Pride in Every Detail

Golden Maple Landscaping was founded on the belief that outdoor spaces should be built with the same care and attention as the homes they surround.

Owner-operator Yorkis personally oversees each project to ensure the highest standards of craftsmanship, communication, and client experience.

With a background rooted in hands-on construction and a commitment to continuous improvement, Yorkis approaches every project with precision, accountability, and pride in the finished result.

For us, this work is more than construction — it is about creating spaces families enjoy for years to come.

Yorkis — Project Builder

Why We Do This Work

A thoughtfully designed outdoor space becomes part of everyday life — where families gather, children play, and memories are made.

We take pride in building spaces that feel welcoming, functional, and enduring.

Because when a space is built properly, it serves families for decades.

A Team You Can Rely On

At Golden Maple Landscaping, we approach outdoor construction the same way we’d want our own homes handled — carefully planned, well built, and clearly communicated.

We work with homeowners who value quality, clear expectations, and a smooth experience from start to finish.

We take on a limited number of projects each season so we can stay focused, organized, and hands-on throughout the build.

Our Work, Backed by Warranty

Our Workmanship Warranty

We stand behind the quality of our work. Depending on the scope and construction tier of your project, our workmanship warranty is up to 10 years.

Warranty coverage is aligned with the level of planning, materials, and construction approach selected, and is clearly outlined as part of the design and build process.
