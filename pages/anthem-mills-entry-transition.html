Anthem Mills Entry Transition

Overview

This project focused on creating a clean, precise transition using porcelain, connecting the entry area to existing site elements. The scope was small and clearly defined, with an emphasis on execution accuracy rather than design exploration.

Scope & Installation Method

  • Porcelain surface installation
  • Open-graded / permeable base system

An open-graded base was selected to support drainage performance while providing proper structural support for the porcelain finish. This approach was appropriate given the site conditions and allowed water to move freely through the system.

Execution

The work was completed exactly to the client’s request, with careful attention to alignment, elevation, and joint consistency. No design phase was required, and the scope remained unchanged throughout the project.

Outcome

The finished transition is clean, durable, and technically sound. The client was extremely satisfied with the clarity of the process and the precision of the installation.
