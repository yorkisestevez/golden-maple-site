How our warranty works

We tailor our workmanship warranty to the level of construction selected:

  • Good: 3-year workmanship warranty
  • Better: 5-year workmanship warranty
  • Best: 10-year workmanship warranty

This approach ensures each project is built, detailed, and supported according to the standards of the tier chosen. Additionally, all manufacturer warranties on materials (such as stone pavers or composite decking) are fully passed on to you, often providing protection for 25+ years.
