Porcelain Patios in Oro-Medonte, thoughtfully designed and built

We create porcelain patios that sit comfortably within rural and estate properties, with layouts designed to match the home and the landscape.

Our planning phase focuses on how you move through the space and where outdoor life naturally happens. By resolving layout early, the finished patio feels cohesive with the property.

Porcelain delivers clean lines and consistent tones, while proper base preparation ensures the patio holds its grade and drainage through seasonal shifts.

What we build

  • Porcelain patios with tight joints and clean edges.
  • Outdoor dining and lounge zones aligned to the home.
  • Integrated steps and landings for grade changes.
  • Drainage-conscious grading and surface transitions.
  • Lighting coordination for evening use.
  • Low-maintenance surfaces with long-term stability.

Our process

  1. Structured intake to confirm fit and project goals.
  2. Planning with materials and layout finalized early.
  3. Scheduled build with detailed base preparation and finish work.
