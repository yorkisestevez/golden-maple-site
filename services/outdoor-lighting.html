The Specifications

Brilliance in the Details.

Low-Voltage LED

We use premium 12V LED systems that consume minimal power while providing warm, high-CRI light that brings out the natural colors of your stone and plants.

Direct Burial Cable

All wiring is commercial-grade direct burial cable, protected in critical areas to ensure your system remains maintenance-free for years.

Cast-Brass Fixtures

We avoid plastic and cheap aluminum. Our fixtures are solid cast brass or copper, designed to patina naturally and never rust or corrode.

Structural Proof

Built for the Long Haul.

In Ontario, the frost line is your biggest enemy. That's why we don't just build on top of the soil—we replace it with a structural engine.

Every Golden Maple project is built on a 12 to 16-inch foundation of open-graded clear stone. This creates a massive void that allows water to move freely without ever freezing and heaving your stone. We build it once, so you never have to build it again.

  • ✓ 16" Engineered Clear Stone Base
  • ✓ Non-Woven Geotextile Separation
  • ✓ Frost-Heave Mitigation
