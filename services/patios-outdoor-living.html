The Specifications

Our Standard Build.

16" Open-Graded Base

Industry standard is 6 inches of granular A. We use 12-16 inches of 3/4" clear aggregate, creating an absolute void for water management and preventing capillary action.

Geotextile Separation

Commercial-grade woven geotextile fabric separates native subsoil from our engineered base, ensuring structural integrity and preventing base contamination over decades.

Polymeric Jointing

Premium polymeric sand installation blocks weed growth and ant infestation while retaining enough flexibility to handle microscopic thermal expansion without cracking.
