The Yorkis Standard

Built for the
Ontario Freeze-Thaw.

Fire features and outdoor seating areas are the most vulnerable parts of a backyard. While industry standards accept 6-inch bases, we mandate a 12 to 16-inch open-graded foundation.

This ensures that your stone remains perfectly level and your fire feature stays safe and stable, even as the ground moves during the spring thaw.

Yorkis's Pro-Tip:

"For fire features, I always recommend natural stone coping. It handles direct heat better than pre-cast materials and looks more authentic as it ages."

Structural Proof

Built for the Long Haul.

In Ontario, the frost line is your biggest enemy. That's why we don't just build on top of the soil—we replace it with a structural engine.

Every Golden Maple project is built on a 12 to 16-inch foundation of open-graded clear stone. This creates a massive void that allows water to move freely without ever freezing and heaving your stone. We build it once, so you never have to build it again.
