The Specifications

Engineered to Hold.

Open-Graded Backfill

We backfill with 3/4" clear stone to ensure 100% drainage, removing the water pressure that is the primary cause of wall failure.

Buried Courses

Every wall starts below grade with a properly compacted leveling pad, providing the lateral resistance needed for long-term stability.

Geogrid Reinforcement

For taller walls, we use commercial-grade geogrid to tie the wall into the soil mass, creating a unified structural block.

Structural Proof

Built for the Long Haul.

In Ontario, the frost line is your biggest enemy. That's why we don't just build on top of the soil—we replace it with a structural engine.

Every Golden Maple project is built on a 12 to 16-inch foundation of open-graded clear stone. This creates a massive void that allows water to move freely without ever freezing and heaving your stone. We build it once, so you never have to build it again.

  • ✓ Non-Woven Geotextile Separation
  • ✓ Frost-Heave Mitigation
