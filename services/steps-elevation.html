The Specifications

Built for the Long Walk.

Monolithic Settings

Large format steps are set on an engineered clear-stone pad, ensuring they act as a single unit and resist shifting during freeze-thaw cycles.

Internal Drainage

Every elevation change acts as a water management point. We build internal drainage behind every riser to prevent ice buildup and stone movement.

Non-Slip Finishes

We select materials with high-friction ratings, ensuring safe passage even in wet or icy conditions, providing peace of mind for all ages.

Structural Proof

Built for the Long Haul.

In Ontario, the frost line is your biggest enemy. That's why we don't just build on top of the soil—we replace it with a structural engine.

Every Golden Maple project is built on a 12 to 16-inch foundation of open-graded clear stone. This creates a massive void that allows water to move freely without ever freezing and heaving your stone. We build it once, so you never have to build it again.

  • ✓ 16" Engineered Clear Stone Base
  • ✓ Non-Woven Geotextile Separation
  • ✓ Frost-Heave Mitigation
