# Golden Maple Landscaping: VIP Build Slot Discovery Call Script

## Opening (2 minutes)
**Tone: Warm, Professional, Exclusive**

---

### 01. Greeting & Purpose

"Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time today. I'm [Your Name] from Golden Maple Landscaping. Before we dive in, I wanted to let you know what we're going to cover in the next 20 minutes.

We've reviewed your project details and specs, and it looks like it's a great fit for what we call our **Master-Build tier**—essentially our most thorough, architecturally-driven approach. This call is really about two things:

1. Making sure we understand your vision and priorities
2. Determining if we're the right team to orchestrate this for you

Think of it as a **mutual fit conversation**. We're pretty selective about which projects we take on—not because we're too busy, but because we want to ensure every project gets our best attention. Sound good?"

---

## Discovery Phase (10 minutes)
**Tone: Curious, Detail-Oriented, Authoritative**

### 02. Project Vision & Priorities

"Let's talk about what success looks like for you. When this project is finished and you're out there for the first time—maybe on a summer evening—what's the moment you're imagining?"

*Listen for emotional drivers. Probe deeper:*
- "Tell me more about that..."
- "What would that feel like?"
- "Is this an entertaining space, a retreat, or both?"

### 03. Understanding Their Timeline & Readiness

"Walk me through your timeline. Are you thinking spring construction, or are you exploring options?"

*Follow-up questions:*
- "Have you thought about the design phase? Many of our clients love doing a 3D Virtual Walkthrough before we break ground—it removes all the guesswork."
- "Are you prepared to move forward this season if everything aligns?"

### 04. Current Challenges & Constraints

"Every property is different. Help me understand your specific situation:
- What's the condition of the yard right now?
- Are there any access challenges, slope issues, or drainage concerns we should know about?
- Have you gotten work done on this property before?"

*Listen for objections, concerns, budget sensitivity.*

---

## Value Positioning (5 minutes)
**Tone: Confident, Risk-Reversing, Premium**

### 05. The Golden Maple Difference

"Here's what's important to understand about how we work:

**We don't just build. We orchestrate.**

Most landscapers start digging. We start designing. We engineer the entire system—drainage, grading, structural load—so your space performs perfectly for a decade, not just a season. We call this our **Master-Build Structural Warranty**, and it's backed by a 10-year guarantee. Most landscapers offer 2-5 years on workmanship. We go further because we know we've built it right.

What that means for you is:
- **Bespoke 3D Design** — You see exactly what you're getting before we touch the ground
- **White-Glove Mobilization** — Seamless coordination from day one
- **Architectural-Grade Execution** — Every detail matters, and we sweat them
- **10-Year Structural Protection** — Peace of mind that extends beyond the season

This isn't for everyone. And honestly? We only take on a select number of projects each year so we can deliver this level of attention."

### 06. The Investment Conversation

"Based on what you've told me—[brief summary of project scope]—we're typically looking at a range of **$[LOW] to $[HIGH]** for a Master-Build approach with premium finishes, full 3D design, and the structural warranty.

Before you react, let me tell you what's *included* in that:
- Full site engineering and grading design
- Premium material selection
- 10-year Master-Build Structural Warranty
- Complete project management and coordination
- Drainage system designed for Canadian seasons

This is different from a quote on a patio from someone online. You're getting the architect of your space, not just the installer."

*Pause for their response. Address objections with:*
- "I get that. What would make this feel like the right investment?"
- "Is it the timeline, the scope, or the financial commitment?"
- "Have you considered the cost of doing this twice? That's what we usually see when people go with the lowest bid."

---

## Next Steps & Soft Close (3 minutes)
**Tone: Consultative, Clear, Decisive**

### 07. Design Phase Proposal

"Here's what happens next if you want to move forward:

**Phase 1: Design & Virtual Immersion ($[DESIGN FEE])**
We'll create a complete 3D design of your space. You'll be able to walk through it virtually, see material options, adjust things in real-time. This fee is fully credited back to your construction contract. This removes all the risk—you know exactly what you're paying for before we break ground.

**Phase 2: Build (Your contract range)**
Once you approve the design, we schedule construction. Typical timelines are 4-8 weeks depending on scope.

I'd like to propose we move forward with a design consultation. Does this Thursday or Friday work better for a site walkthrough?"

*If hesitation:*
"I know this is a big decision. What's the one thing that would help you feel more confident moving forward?"

---

## Closing
"Awesome. Let me get that scheduled. You'll hear from our office by EOD today with confirmation. In the meantime, think about any photos of spaces you love—we love reference images. And don't hesitate to call if questions come up. We're here to make this effortless."

---

## Key Objection Handling

### "Can you give me a ballpark price right now?"
**Response:** "I could, but here's the thing—outdoor projects are too variable. Soil conditions, access, what's underground, drainage—these all change the equation. What we *can* do is get boots on the ground for a 30-minute site review. From there, I'll have way better intel. Would that work?"

### "We want to get 3 quotes first"
**Response:** "That makes total sense. One thing I'd ask: ask the other companies the same questions we're asking—what's included in site engineering? Do they design before they build? What's their warranty? We're betting that once you compare apples to apples, you'll see the value in what we're doing. And honestly? A proper design consultation is worth it regardless."

### "This is more expensive than I thought"
**Response:** "I hear you. A couple of things: One, most folks underestimate the cost of doing it right. Two, the Design Phase is actually a great place to work backward from budget. Let's get the design locked, and then we can talk about value engineering—premium finishes, phased approach, whatever works. Does a design consultation make sense from there?"
