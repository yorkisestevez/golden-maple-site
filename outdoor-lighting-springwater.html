Outdoor Lighting in Springwater, thoughtfully designed and built

We design lighting plans that extend the use of your outdoor spaces with warmth, safety, and quiet atmosphere.

Lighting is planned alongside the hardscape and planting layout so fixtures feel integrated rather than added after the fact. We focus on soft layers that guide movement without harsh glare.

Every installation balances aesthetics and reliability, with clean wiring routes and thoughtful placement that respects the architecture and landscape.

What we build

  • Pathway and step lighting for safe circulation.
  • Feature lighting for trees, walls, and architectural elements.
  • Warm ambient zones for patios and gathering areas.
  • Subtle edge lighting for grade changes.
  • Discreet wiring and transformer placement.
  • Fixture planning aligned with landscape design.

Our process

  1. Structured intake to confirm fit and project goals.
  2. Lighting plan and fixture strategy built into the layout.
  3. Installation with clean routing and final nighttime calibration.
